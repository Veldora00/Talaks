Your agreement with us is made up of two things: these terms, and the plan agreement issued when your order is approved, which sets out your device, term, monthly payment, early-exit charge and return date. If the two ever conflict, your plan agreement wins.

1. How a Talaks plan works

A Talaks plan is a fixed-term subscription to use a device. It is not a purchase, a rent-to-own or lease-to-own arrangement, a loan, or any other form of credit. You choose your term when you order. Most devices offer 12 or 24 months, and some offer 36 — the terms available are shown on each device’s page, because they differ by device.

Talaks remains the owner of the device for the whole term, and at the end of it. You do not acquire any right, title or interest in the device at any point, and there is no option to buy it — not during the term, not at the end, not by paying anything extra.

Total payments across your term are set at or below the device’s recommended retail price at the time you order. No interest is charged, and there is no establishment fee, deposit or bond.

2. Eligibility and identity verification

To hold a Talaks plan you must be at least 18, live in Australia, and have an Australian delivery address. Devices are not shipped outside Australia.

Approval requires a successful identity check, carried out through our verification provider (currently Didit). It involves a photo of your identity document and a short liveness check, both handled on their secure service rather than ours.

Documents we can accept include an Australian passport, an Australian driver licence, or a foreign passport. Where a document can be checked automatically and the result is clear, verification usually finishes in a couple of minutes.

If a document cannot be verified automatically — the photo is unclear, the document is an unusual type, or the check is inconclusive — your order goes to manual review instead of being refused. We may then ask you for a second form of identification or a supporting document before we decide. We will always tell you exactly what we need and why.

We do not run a credit check to approve an order, and applying does not create an enquiry on your credit file.

Meeting these requirements does not guarantee approval. Every order is assessed individually and we may decline one without giving reasons — though we will always tell you that we have, and nothing will have been charged.

3. Placing an order and our approval

Submitting an order is a request, not a completed agreement. Nothing is charged when you place it. Your card details are validated and stored by our payment provider, Stripe, and no payment is taken.

We review every order before accepting it, usually within one business day. If we accept it, we take your first monthly payment at that point, issue your plan agreement, and prepare the device for delivery. If we decline it, no payment is taken and there is nothing for you to do.

You can withdraw an order at any time while it is still being reviewed, from your account page, at no cost. Your agreement with us begins only when we accept the order and take the first payment.

4. Changing your mind

You have 14 days from the day your device is delivered to change your mind — counted from delivery, not from when you ordered, so waiting for the post never eats into it. Tell us within that window and return the device unused and undamaged, in its original packaging with all accessories, and we will cancel the plan and refund the payment you have made.

You cover the cost of returning the device to us unless it arrived faulty or was not what you ordered, in which case we cover it. If the device comes back damaged or with parts missing, we may deduct the cost of putting that right from your refund.

This is in addition to any rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law, not instead of them.

5. Delivery

Delivery is free within Australia and usually takes three to five business days from approval. We will give you a tracking reference once the device is posted.

Delivery timeframes are estimates. We will keep you informed if something is delayed, but we are not responsible for delays caused by the carrier, weather, or other things outside our control.

Please check the device when it arrives and tell us within 48 hours if anything is damaged, missing, or not what you ordered.

6. Payments, fees and charges

Your monthly payment is fixed for the length of your term and shown in full before you order. It covers the device and nothing else — there is no insurance, cover, protection plan or service subscription bundled into it, and no mobile service or SIM is included.

Payments are taken automatically from the card you provided, on the same date each month, starting on the day we approve your order. Pricing is set from the device’s retail price at the time you order and does not change part-way through your term, whatever happens to the retail price afterwards.

What that looks like in practice. Take a device with a retail price of $1,000, on a plan of $40 a month over 24 months — $960 across the whole term:

Leaving early at month 8. Sixteen payments remain, so the early-exit charge is 16 × $40 = $640. Pay that, post the device back within 14 days, and you are done. You have paid $960 in total — the same as finishing the term, just sooner.

Not returning the device at the end of the term. The non-return fee is $1,000 − $480 (half of the $960 plan total) = $520. It is the same figure whenever it applies.

Losing the device at month 8. The plan ends. You owe the remaining 16 payments ($640) plus the non-return fee ($520): $1,160, on top of the $320 already paid. Across the plan that is $1,480 — more than the device cost new. That is deliberate: not returning a device is never the cheaper option.

The non-return fee is a fixed figure for your plan, known from day one. The early-exit charge falls every month you stay. Both are set out in your plan agreement before you confirm it.

7. Missed payments and hardship

If a payment fails we will retry it and let you know. We would much rather hear from you than chase you.

If you are having trouble paying, contact us early. We will work with you on a payment arrangement, a pause, or another way through. Talk to us before a payment fails if you can — it is far easier to arrange something in advance than to unwind arrears afterwards.

If payments remain unpaid and you have not made an arrangement with us, we may refer the amount to a debt collection agency and recover the reasonable costs of doing so.

8. Looking after the device

The device is ours, and you are looking after it for the length of your plan. While you have it, you agree to:

  • use it normally and carefully, and keep it in good working order;
  • keep the original box and all included accessories until the plan ends;
  • not sell, hire out, pawn, give away, or offer the device as security for anything;
  • not modify it, remove or deface serial numbers or IMEI markings, or have it repaired by anyone other than the manufacturer or an authorised repairer;
  • not take it permanently out of Australia, and tell us if you move address;
  • not use it for anything unlawful;
  • keep your own backups of anything on it — we are not responsible for your data.

9. Ownership and warranty

Talaks owns the device throughout your plan and afterwards. You have no right, title or interest in it, and it must never be treated as yours to dispose of.

The manufacturer’s warranty covers hardware faults during your term. You have two ways to use it, and either is fine:

  • Take it in yourself — an Apple Store, a Samsung service centre, or any repairer authorised by the manufacturer. Tell us first so we know the device is out, and send us the job reference when it is done.
  • Let us arrange it — tell us what is wrong and we will organise it and post you a prepaid satchel.

Either way a warranty fault costs you nothing. Please do not use an unauthorised repairer: it voids the manufacturer warranty and makes the repair your responsibility in full.

Nothing in these terms excludes, restricts or modifies any guarantee, right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law. Those rights apply in addition to everything written here, and where anything in these terms conflicts with them, the law prevails.

10. Damage, loss and theft

We do not sell, include, or imply any insurance product. This section sets out plainly who pays for what.

Faults that are not your doing. If the device fails through a manufacturing or quality defect, you pay nothing. We arrange the repair or replacement and carry the cost.

Accidental damage. Cracked screens, liquid damage and similar accidents are your responsibility. Tell us and we will arrange the repair through the manufacturer or an authorised repairer and pass on exactly what it costs us — no markup and no service fee on top. We will always quote you before any work is booked, and you may decline and arrange your own authorised repair instead.

Ordinary wear and tear is never charged. Scuffs, light scratches and normal ageing are expected across a term and cost you nothing at return.

Loss and theft are not covered. If the device is lost or stolen you remain responsible for it. Your plan ends, the remaining payments on your term become payable, and the non-return fee in section 13 applies on top. Report it to the police and to us as soon as you can, and give us the police report number — we will help where we can. If you want protection against loss or theft, a contents or standalone device insurance policy is the right tool, and we would encourage you to consider one.

Deliberate damage and misuse. Damage caused deliberately, by misuse, or by unauthorised repair or modification is your responsibility in full, at the actual cost of repair or, where the device cannot reasonably be repaired, the non-return fee.

11. Ending your plan early

You can end your plan before the end of its term by telling us, paying the early-exit charge, and returning the device under section 12 within 14 days.

The early-exit charge is the monthly payments remaining on your term, paid in one amount. Nothing is added and nothing is discounted: you pay what you would have paid had you stayed, no later than the day you leave. The figure is shown to you before you commit to leaving and falls every month you remain. Section 6 has a worked example.

Thinking of moving to a different device instead? Email info@talaks.com before you cancel. We will tell you what is left on your current plan and what a new one would look like.

12. End of term and returning the device

We will contact you before your term ends. You then have three choices:

  • Return it — send the device back and your plan simply ends. Your payments stop.
  • Extend — continue on your current device at the rate we offer you at the time.
  • Upgrade — return this device and start a fresh plan on a newer one. There is no upgrade fee.

You must post the device within 14 days of your term ending, using the prepaid label we provide. What matters is the date you lodge it with the carrier, not the date it reaches us — keep the receipt, and once it is scanned in, delays in transit are ours to worry about, not yours. Before you send it, please:

  • back up anything you want to keep;
  • sign out and turn off Find My iPhone, Activation Lock, Google FRP or the equivalent — a device still locked to your account cannot be accepted and will be treated as not returned until it is unlocked;
  • factory reset it and remove your SIM or eSIM;
  • include the box and all accessories.

We check the device when it arrives and let you know the outcome. If there is damage beyond fair wear and tear, we will tell you what it is and what the repair costs before charging anything.

13. Non-return fee

If your plan ends, or is ended early, and the device has not been posted back within 14 days, and you have not agreed something else with us, a non-return fee applies. It is the device’s recommended retail price at the time you ordered, less 50% of the total subscription payments across your full term. It is a fixed figure for your plan, stated in your plan agreement, and does not change with how far through the term you are.

The fee is a one-off amount, separate from your monthly payments. It is due within 14 days of our written notice, in addition to any monthly payments still owing on your term — it does not replace them and it is not spread across the months. The same fee applies where a device is lost or stolen, or comes back so damaged that it cannot reasonably be repaired. Worked examples are in section 6.

Paying the non-return fee does not buy the device. It compensates us for a device that was not returned; the device remains our property, and if it is later found or returned we will refund the fee less our actual costs and any reduction in its value.

If the device is lost or stolen, tell us and the police straight away. Once the remaining payments and the non-return fee are settled, nothing further is owed.

We will always try to reach you and sort something out first. This is a last resort, not a first step.

You are in default if you do not pay what is owed and have not agreed an arrangement with us, if you breach section 8, if information you gave us was false, if the device is used unlawfully, or if you permanently leave Australia without telling us.

If that happens and we cannot resolve it with you, we may end your plan, require the device to be returned, charge the non-return fee if it is not, and recover reasonable enforcement costs. Where a device is not returned after we have asked for it in writing, we may report it as retained without authority and ask the carrier network to block its IMEI.

We will always give you written notice and a reasonable opportunity to fix things first.
