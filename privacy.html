Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Talaks Trading (ABN 52 477 928 344), Sydney, Australia

Talaks Trading (we, us, our) handles personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with (including overseas), how long we keep it, and how to make a complaint.

What we collect

To assess an application and run your plan, we collect: your name, contact details, and delivery address; the outcome of an identity check carried out by our identity verification provider, Didit; payment information, handled directly by our payment processor, Stripe — Talaks never receives or stores your full card number; and details of the device, plan, and term you select.

Identity documents and biometric data specifically: the photo of your ID and the liveness check happen entirely on Didit’s own hosted page, not ours. No one at Talaks sees the photo of your document or any biometric or facial data, and we do not store them. Didit sends its result to our system automatically, and our system keeps only a short receipt of it: the name on your document, the document type, issuing country and how it was checked, a yes/no confirmation that you are over 18, and whether each check passed. Everything else Didit sends — your document number, your date of birth, and the links to the scans, portrait and liveness video — is discarded at that moment and never written down on our side. Your date of birth itself is never stored; only the over-18 answer is. The full record stays with Didit.

How we use it

  • To verify your identity and assess your application.
  • To set up, bill, and manage your plan.
  • To arrange delivery, returns, and device repairs.
  • To contact you about your plan, including payment and return reminders.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations, including under the National Credit Code.

Who we share it with, and overseas disclosure

We share the minimum necessary information with the service providers who help us operate, and are upfront that this means some of your information is handled or stored outside Australia as a normal part of how these providers run their services:

  • Didit (identity verification) — confirms who you are; document images and biometric checks are processed on Didit’s own infrastructure, not ours.
  • Stripe (payments) — processes your payment and stores your card details; Stripe operates globally, including in the United States.
  • Supabase (our database provider) — stores your account, order, and plan records. Our database is hosted in Supabase’s Mumbai, India region (AWS ap-south-1), not in Australia.
  • Resend (email) — sends transactional emails about your plan (confirmations, shipping, payment notices) on our behalf.
  • Delivery partners, to get your device to you.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Where we disclose information overseas, we rely on these providers’ own privacy and security commitments; we do not currently have a separate contractual data-handling agreement layered on top of each provider’s standard terms.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your plan is active, and afterwards for as long as we're required to by law (including tax and financial record-keeping obligations, typically several years) or need it to resolve a dispute. Identity verification results are kept for as long as your account exists, so we can show we met our obligations if ever asked to.

Your rights, and how to complain

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask how it's been used. To make a request, email info@talaks.com.au and we will respond within a reasonable time, generally within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with how we've handled your personal information or a request, tell us first at the same email address so we can put it right. If you remain unsatisfied, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), the independent regulator for privacy in Australia.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, and rely on established, security-audited providers (Supabase, Stripe, Didit) for the systems that store or process sensitive data rather than building our own.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how your data is handled can be sent to info@talaks.com.au.
