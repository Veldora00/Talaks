Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Talaks Pty Ltd, Sydney, Australia

Draft, not yet legally reviewed. This page describes how Talaks intends to handle your data in plain language. Before this goes live for real customers — especially once identity verification and payments are connected — it needs a proper review against the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) by a qualified professional.

What we collect

To assess an application and run your subscription, we collect: your name, contact details, and delivery address; identity verification information (via our identity verification provider, Didit) such as a photo ID and a liveness check; payment information, handled directly by our payment processor (Stripe) — Talaks does not store your full card details; and details of the device, plan, and term you select.

How we use it

  • To verify your identity and assess your application.
  • To set up, bill, and manage your subscription.
  • To arrange delivery, returns, and device repairs.
  • To contact you about your plan, including payment and return reminders.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary information with the service providers who help us operate: our identity verification provider (Didit) to confirm who you are, our payment processor (Stripe) to process payments, and delivery partners to get your device to you. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your subscription is active, and afterwards for as long as we're required to by law (including tax and financial record-keeping obligations) or need it to resolve a dispute.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to explain how it's been used. To make a request, email info@talaks.com.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, and rely on established, security-audited providers (Supabase, Stripe, Didit) for the systems that store or process sensitive data rather than building our own.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how your data is handled can be sent to info@talaks.com.
