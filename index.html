Questions

Straight answers before you commit.

Can I keep the phone instead of returning it?

Not with a subscription, no — the phone goes back to us at the end of your term. That's actually the whole trick behind the price: because we keep ownership and give each phone a second life with its next subscriber, we can charge you less than it'd cost to buy outright. If you love always having the newest model without the big upfront cost, this is exactly how it's meant to work.

Do I own the phone at the end of my plan?

Talaks stays the owner for the life of the plan. When your term's up, just return it, renew, or hop onto the newest model — no buy-out option, which is exactly what keeps things simple and keeps your monthly price low.

Do you run a credit check?

No credit check — just a quick ID verification so we know you are who you say you are. It usually takes under two minutes and won't touch your credit file.

Can I end my subscription early?

Sure — life happens. You pay the payments left on your term in one go, send the phone back, and that's it. The figure is shown before you sign up and falls every month you stay.

What if I don't return the phone?

We'll always reach out first to sort something out. If a device genuinely can't be returned or resolved, a Non-Return Fee applies — a fixed figure in your plan agreement, set so keeping the phone always costs more than returning it. It's a last resort, not our first move.

What if I damage it?

If it stopped working through a manufacturing fault, that’s on us and you pay nothing. If you dropped it, that one’s yours — but we’ll organise the repair through the manufacturer or an authorised repairer and charge you what it actually costs us, quoted before anything is booked. No markup, no service fee, and no monthly insurance you didn’t ask for. Loss and theft aren’t covered.

Do I need to keep the original box and accessories?

Just hang onto the box and any included accessories (like the cable) until your subscription ends — you'll need them when you return, swap, or upgrade. If something's missing, a small replacement fee may apply.

Can I swap to a different phone partway through my plan?

Once your current term wraps up, absolutely — swap to whatever's newest, free, no upgrade fee. Want to swap sooner, because something better just launched? Email us and we'll lay out what's left on your current plan and what a new one would look like.

Can I subscribe to more than one device at a time?

Yep — plenty of people run two plans at once, say a phone for themselves and one for a partner. Each device just sits on its own separate plan.

Is this the same as buying on a payment plan?

Not quite. On a payment plan you're buying the phone, and what's left to pay is a debt you owe. With Talaks you're subscribing to use it. Your total payments stay under the phone's retail price, and once you send it back at the end of your term, you're done.

Plan terms, in plain language

  1. Your plan runs for a fixed term you choose at sign-up — 12 or 24 months, and 36 on some devices. Talaks remains the legal owner of the device for the entire term.
  2. Total payments across the term are set at or below the device's retail cash price at the time you sign up. No interest is charged.
  3. Approval requires an identity verification check. Meeting our minimum requirements doesn't guarantee approval — every application is assessed individually.
  4. You may end your plan early by paying the remaining payments on your term in one amount and returning the device within 14 days.
  5. At the end of your term, return the device in good working condition within the return window stated in your agreement, extend your plan, or start a new plan on a new device.
  6. A Non-Return Fee applies if the device isn't returned and no alternative arrangement is agreed: the device's retail price at order less 50% of your total plan payments, in addition to any payments still owing.
  7. If the device fails through a manufacturing fault you pay nothing. Accidental damage is your responsibility: we arrange the repair through the manufacturer or an authorised repairer and pass on the actual cost, quoted to you first. Loss and theft are not covered — full conditions are in your plan agreement.
  8. These summary terms are for guidance only. Your full plan agreement, provided at sign-up, is the governing document.
